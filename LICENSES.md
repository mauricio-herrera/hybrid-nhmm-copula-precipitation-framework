## Precipitation data
The compressed data file in `Data/` is a processed research input rather than
original software. The MIT software license does **not** supersede the rights,
licenses, or attribution requirements of the underlying data.

The manuscript identifies CR2MET as the source of the gridded Chilean
precipitation product. CR2 provides this data with Creative Commons 4.0
licensing information, and requests citation of:

Boisier, Juan P. (2023). *CR2MET: A high-resolution precipitation and
temperature dataset for the period 1960–2021 in continental Chile* (v2.5)
[Data set]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7529682

Original product and licensing information:
https://www.cr2.cl/datos-productos-grillados/

The CSV additionally includes derived identifiers, regime labels, and other
processed variables. Attribution to other underlying sources, where applicable,
should be retained. This repository does not assert that the CR2MET citation
alone documents the provenance of every field in the processed CSV.
